# Sikke Paper-Collage Style Bible

The fixed visual language for every Sikke visual and reel. It comes from the Story Studio paper-collage bible, rebuilt for Sikke's brand: engraved coins, old banknotes, ledger paper, a magazine's cut-and-paste feel. Paste the Style Anchor into every image and video prompt so all shots look like one piece of work.

## 1. The look in one paragraph

Handmade paper collage with a museum-catalogue edge. Hand-cut paper cutouts with visible deckled edges and paper fibre, layered at different depths with soft real drop shadows. Mix of matte ledger paper, kraft card, engraved-style line drawings of coins and banknotes, halftone print dots, and archival stamps. A single warm light from top-left. Motion, where used, is stop-motion at 12 fps with slight jitter. Nothing glossy, nothing neon.

## 2. Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper base | Sikke paper | #EDF1EE | Background sheet |
| Ink | Sikke ink | #17262A | Outlines, headlines, engraving lines |
| Main 1 | Sikke teal | #0D5E57 | Primary blocks, cutout shapes |
| Main 2 | Ledger kraft | #B08A5B | Secondary paper, card stock, texture |
| Accent | Sikke gold | #8E6A22 | The one thing the viewer must look at (a coin, a number, a stamp) |

Rule: one paper base + two main colours + one accent per shot. Gold only on the hero element. Nothing outside this palette, so the look stays recognisable across editions.

## 3. Style Anchor (paste into every image prompt)

```
Handmade paper-collage style: hand-cut paper cutouts with visible deckled edges and fine paper fibre texture, layered at different depths with soft realistic drop shadows, matte ledger paper and kraft card, engraved-style ink line details of coins and banknotes, halftone print dots, subtle film grain, one warm soft light from top-left, museum-catalogue feel, tactile and slightly imperfect, palette of warm paper #EDF1EE, deep teal #0D5E57, kraft brown #B08A5B, ink #17262A and one gold accent #8E6A22.
```

Motion add-on (video prompts only):

```
Stop-motion at 12 fps with slight frame-to-frame jitter; paper pieces slide, fold, flip or pop up in small stepped moves, like a hand-animated collage.
```

## 4. Characters and objects

Sikke stories are about money, not people's faces. Prefer the object as the character:

- The coin, the banknote, the ledger, the certificate, the stamp, the scale, the key.
- A simple paper figure only when the story needs a person: a flat paper silhouette with no face detail, or a generic figure with a halftone head. Never a real person's likeness, and never an identifiable portrait of a named historical or living figure.

Write a Character Card once if a figure recurs:

```
PAPER FIGURE [NAME]: a flat cut-paper silhouette of a [man/woman/figure] in [garment], no facial features, [kraft / teal] paper, a [prop] in hand, thin ink outline.
```

## 5. Typography in frame

- Headline words as cut-out letters, ransom-note style, or typewriter text on a torn paper strip. Serif display letters for headlines (Bodoni-like), in ink or on a gold strip.
- 1–4 words per frame, in the target language. Add Farsi and Turkish text in the edit, not in the model.

## 6. Motion grammar (video only)

Allowed: slide in or out from an edge; pop-up fold from flat; flip to reveal the back; tear reveal to the next scene; paper parallax between layers; unfold a map, letter or chart; stamp drop with a small bounce; slow push-in or lateral truck across a paper table.

Avoid: smooth CGI motion, glass or liquid effects, morphing, realistic human motion, shattering objects.

## 7. Sound (for the edit and the music brief)

Paper rustle, scissor snips, a stamp thud, coin clinks used as light percussion, a soft ledger page turn on each cut. Music: see `music.md`.

## 8. Negative line (images and video)

```
Avoid: photorealism, glossy 3D render, neon, glass or plastic look, smooth CGI motion, morphing shapes, warped faces, extra limbs, real people's faces, real brand logos, watermark, subtitles, gibberish text, cluttered layout, oversaturated colours.
```

## 9. Sikke-specific visual rules

- Every money story gets one hero object (a coin, a note, a stamp, a certificate) as the anchor.
- Allegations are shown as allegations: a "CLAIMED" tag, a question mark, an unsealed document, never a verdict stamp.
- Regional stories (Cyprus, Türkiye, Iran): show places, objects and documents only. No flags, no political symbols, no maps that imply a border position.
- Ancient and medieval stories: use engraved-coin and museum-object motifs, dates in Gregorian with the Solar Hijri year where the text uses one.
- If a visual needs a real archival image, the licence and source go in the brief (see SKILL.md step 6).
