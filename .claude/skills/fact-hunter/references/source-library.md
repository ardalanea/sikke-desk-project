# Source library

Tiers decide how much a source counts in fact-verify.

- **Tier A (primary / institutional):** court records, regulator releases, central banks, museums, national archives, peer-reviewed research, official statistics.
- **Tier B (established press / specialist):** major newspapers and wire services, specialist trackers with stated methods, academic books.
- **Tier C (leads only):** Wikipedia, listicles, blogs, social media, YouTube, AI summaries. Never the only source for a published fact.

- Smithsonian National Numismatic Collection — americanhistory.si.edu
- American Numismatic Society, incl. MANTIS and Nomisma — numismatics.org
