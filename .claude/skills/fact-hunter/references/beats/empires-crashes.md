# Beat: Empires & Crashes (1500–1999)

**ID prefix:** HIS
**Covers:** Spanish silver and the price revolution, tulip mania, Mississippi Company and John Law, South Sea Bubble, birth of central banks, gold standard, Ottoman debt and the Ottoman Public Debt Administration, Imperial Bank of Persia, 1907 and 1929, Weimar and Hungary hyperinflations, Bretton Woods, Nixon shock, 1987, 1992 Black Wednesday, Asian crisis 1997, Russia 1998, Argentina 2001.
**Best sources:** Federal Reserve History, Bank of England, MeasuringWorth, Hanke–Krus table, academic histories, period newspapers.
**Golden angles:** the one person who saw it coming, absurd price numbers, how ordinary people lived through it, a law still in force today because of it.
**Watch out for:** overstated bubble myths (tulip mania's scale is debated by historians — say so), inflation figures quoted without source or period.
