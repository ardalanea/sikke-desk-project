# Beat: Crypto & Fintech

**ID prefix:** CRY
**Covers:** pre-Bitcoin digital cash (DigiCash, e-gold), Bitcoin's firsts, Mt. Gox, The DAO, ICO boom, Terra/Luna, Celsius, FTX, DeFi hacks and bridge exploits, rug pulls, lost keys and buried hard drives, stablecoins, CBDCs, mobile money (M-Pesa), card and payments history.
**Best sources:** court filings, DefiLlama, Rekt, Chainalysis/TRM reports, Web3 Is Going Great, the Bitcoin white paper, established press.
**Golden angles:** records and firsts, mind-bending "what it would be worth today", how a hack actually happened in plain words, scams that copy old frauds.
**Watch out for:** never recommend or promote any coin, exchange or service; price-based "worth today" figures must state the date and price source; trackers disagree on counts — name the tracker.
