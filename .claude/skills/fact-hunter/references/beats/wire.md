# Beat: Wire (this week's money news)

**ID prefix:** WIR
**Covers:** central bank decisions, big fraud charges and sentences, crypto hacks over $10M, record auctions of coins and notes, new banknote designs, notable currency moves, regulator warnings.
**Best sources:** the regulator or court itself first, then wire services and established press. For crypto hacks, DefiLlama plus one press or on-chain source.
**Golden angles:** the news item plus its history echo ("this happened before in…"), pass it to the editor as a peg.
**Watch out for:** fast-moving numbers (date-stamp every figure "as of"), unconfirmed rumours, anything that reads as trading advice. Wire cards go stale: mark them timely and expire after 2 weeks unless re-verified.
