# Beat: Our Region (Cyprus, Türkiye, Iran, Middle East)

**ID prefix:** REG
**Covers:** Persian money from the daric to the rial and toman, Imperial Bank of Persia, Iranian banknote history, Ottoman akçe and kuruş, Ottoman Bank, Galata bankers, Turkish lira redenominations, Cypriot money from ancient copper to the Cyprus pound and euro, North Cyprus's use of the Turkish lira, Gulf currencies, hawala, bazaar finance.
**Best sources:** TCMB, Central Bank of Iran and its Money Museum, Central Bank of Cyprus, SALT Research, Encyclopaedia Iranica, Turkish and Farsi academic and press archives. Search in Turkish and Farsi, not only English.
**Golden angles:** facts the local audience will be proud or amazed to learn, everyday money words with surprising origins, then-and-now prices.
**Watch out for:** political neutrality is mandatory (Cyprus question, Iranian politics and sanctions, Turkish domestic politics). Report facts and dates; never take sides, never mock a currency's current weakness. Escalate to the editor if a story needs political context to make sense.
