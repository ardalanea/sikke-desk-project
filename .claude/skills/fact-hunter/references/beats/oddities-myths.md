# Beat: Oddities & Myths

**ID prefix:** ODD
**Covers:** strange currencies (rai stones, katanga crosses, squirrel pelts, prison money), record banknotes and coins, misprints, money in art and design, money superstitions, and famous money myths checked (misattributed quotes like Einstein on compound interest, "salary from salt", "the Rothschilds and Waterloo" legend).
**Best sources:** museums, numismatic societies, Quote Investigator for quotes, academic folklore and history sources.
**Golden angles:** "wait, really?" facts; myth-busters framed as "you've heard X; here's what actually happened".
**Watch out for:** myths that carry antisemitic or ethnic conspiracy baggage — only run these as clear debunks with care, or skip. Mark the card status Myth when the claim is false.
