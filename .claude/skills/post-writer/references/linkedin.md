# LinkedIn

- **Weight:** Heavy, 180–300 words. Limit is 3,000 characters; only the first 2–3 lines show before "see more", so they must hook a professional.
- **Angle:** the business, risk or leadership lesson inside the story (trust, incentives, due diligence, liquidity, regulation, innovation). History as a mirror for today's decisions.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook in 1–2 lines (a surprising fact or a counter-intuitive claim).
  2. Short story in 4–6 one- to two-line paragraphs.
  3. "What it teaches us" — 2–3 crisp takeaways (prose or a short list).
  4. One question to invite professional comments.
  5. Source line, then 3–5 hashtags (#Sikke #TEAMedia #FinancialHistory …).
- **Tone:** calm, intelligent, no hype, no emojis or at most one.
- Posting from the TEA Media page or from the founders' personal profiles both work; personal profiles usually reach more people. Note the intended account in the edition pack.
