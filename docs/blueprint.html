Sikke Desk

A · The brief

What the desk produces

Telegram & WhatsApp channels

Daily text posts with a strong first line, the fact, and a source line. Longer story threads for the bigger cases.

Instagram & Facebook

Carousel scripts (slide by slide), captions, hashtags and short reel scripts, each with a design brief.

LinkedIn

Fewer, deeper posts with a business lesson drawn from the story, in a professional voice.

Every verified fact saved as a structured card, tagged and searchable, so nothing is researched twice.

C · Workflow

How a fact travels to the page

Editor-in-Chief + Assignment Editor

Budget the issue

Pick the week's theme or news peg, list anniversaries, check the archive for gaps, and hand each desk a short assignment.

Beat reporters, in parallel

Dig

Each desk searches its source list and returns 8–15 candidate facts as fact cards, each with at least one source link.

Editor-in-Chief

Shortlist

Score each card for surprise, relevance and freshness. Drop duplicates and anything already in the archive.

Fact-Check Desk + Numbers Desk

Verify

An agent that never saw the research re-checks every claim, date and figure. Cards end as Verified, Needs second source, or Killed.

Archive Librarian

File

Verified cards go into the archive with full tags, whether or not they are used this week.

Copy Desk + Art Desk

Write

Posts for each platform are drafted from verified cards only, each with a design brief for the carousel slide or image.

Language Desk

Localise

Farsi and Turkish editions, adapted for each audience, with local examples where they fit.

Standards Editor → you

Sign off

Legal and ethics pass, then the issue comes to you for final approval before anything is published.

H · House rules

Standards every agent follows

Two sources or it doesn't run. At least one should be institutional, academic or primary.

Allegation is not guilt. Use "charged", "convicted" or "settled" exactly as the record says.

No investment advice. We tell stories about money; we never tell readers what to buy.

Old money gets context. Every historic sum shows the original amount plus a dated, sourced modern estimate.

Precise dates. "c." for approximate dates; give ranges where historians disagree.

Myths get labelled. A famous but false story can run, as a myth-buster.

Images need a licence. Public domain, Creative Commons with credit, or our own.

Same facts in every language. Localisation may change examples and tone, never figures or claims.

J · Decided

Decisions so far

Brand: Sikke by TEA Media. "Sikke" means coin in Turkish and Farsi (سکه). Tagline: money's strangest true stories.

All three languages everywhere. Every edition goes out in English, Farsi and Turkish on every platform.

Two editions a week. One lead story each, written for Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook (carousel, caption, reel script) and LinkedIn.

Tone follows the reader-load rule. Every post is a gym weight: grip, reps, rest, finish. Length decides the tone, and the tone fits the story.

Full team, scalable pace. All 8 desks are built. At 2 a week the editor rotates 3 desks per edition and stocks the archive; it scales to daily without rebuilding.

Approval: Ardalan or Niloofar. Either founder can approve an edition. Nothing is posted without one of you.

Decide later: how to handle sensitive regional politics. Until then, the desk flags those stories and asks before writing.
