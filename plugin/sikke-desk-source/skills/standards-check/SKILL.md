# Standards Check

Read the whole trilingual edition pack. Report **Blocking** issues (must fix before approval) and **Advisory** notes.

## Blocking

1. **Legal status.** Every named person in a crime or fraud story is described exactly as the record allows: alleged / charged / convicted / acquitted / settled. No "fraudster" or "thief" for anyone not convicted. Check all three languages.
2. **Living private people.** Ordinary (non-public) living people named without a clear public-record reason → remove the name.
3. **Financial advice.** No buy/sell/hold language, price predictions, "this coin will…", or recommendations of any exchange, coin, product or bank.
4. **Conflict of interest.** No mention or promotion of any company TEA Media works for or is linked to, unless the user explicitly approves and it is labelled.
5. **Political neutrality.** Cyprus question, Turkish and Iranian politics, sanctions: facts only, neutral wording, no mockery of any country's currency or government, no loaded labels. If neutrality is impossible, escalate.
6. **Panic risk.** Nothing that implies a specific current bank, exchange or currency is about to fail or freeze.
7. **Cross-language consistency.** Numbers, dates and claim strength identical in EN, FA and TR.
8. **Unsourced claims.** Any claim not on the verified card.
9. **Images.** Every planned image has a licence or is original/AI-made and labelled as an illustration where it could be mistaken for a historical photo.

## Advisory

- Hate or stereotype risk (e.g. myths with ethnic or religious undertones).
- Sensitive suffering (victims of crashes, suicides linked to frauds — omit details).
- Platform rules (no misleading cover slides, no engagement bait like "comment YES").
- Tone mismatch with sikke-voice.

## Output

```
Standards report — <edition id>
Blocking: <none | list with exact fix applied or required>
Advisory: <list>
Cleared for user approval: yes / no
```
