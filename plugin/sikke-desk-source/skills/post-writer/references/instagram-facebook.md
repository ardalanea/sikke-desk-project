# Instagram and Facebook

One carousel serves both platforms; captions differ slightly.

## Carousel (Medium weight)

- **Slides:** 7–10 (platform allows up to 20; more than 10 loses readers). Portrait 4:5 (1080×1350).
- **Words per slide:** max 25, ideally 8–18. Big type, one idea.
- **Arc:**
  1. **Cover:** the grip line as a headline (max 10 words) + series label. This slide decides everything.
  2. **Setup:** where and when, as a scene.
  3–6. **Build:** each slide reveals one step and ends on a turn ("Then the letters stopped coming.").
  7–8. **Twist / payoff:** the surprising detail, the number made physical.
  9. **Echo:** what it means today or the lesson.
  10. **Close:** "Save this · Share with someone who…" + sources in small text + Sikke logo.
- Write the visual for every slide (see visual-brief).

## Caption

- **Instagram:** 80–150 words. First 125 characters show before "more", so the hook goes there. Then 2–3 short paragraphs adding one detail not on the slides, a question for comments, source line, then 5–8 hashtags (limit is 30; use few, relevant ones: #Sikke #TEAMedia #MoneyHistory #FinanceFacts + topic tags; FA and TR versions use local-language hashtags too).
- **Facebook:** same story, 80–200 words, can be more conversational; 2–3 hashtags only.

## Reel script (Feather/Light, 30–45 seconds)

- ~80–110 spoken words in EN (FA and TR run a little longer; keep timing).
- Format as a table: time | voiceover | on-screen text (max 6 words) | shot/visual.
- 0–2 s: the grip as on-screen text and voice at once. Change visual every 2–4 s. End on the twist, then a 2-second end card.
- Voiceover can be produced later with TEA Media's Farsi voices.
