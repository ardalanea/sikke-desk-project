# Visual Brief

## Sikke look (default until a brand kit replaces it)

- Feel: a museum archive meets a modern newsroom. Banknote engraving textures, coin close-ups, old ledger paper, set against clean modern type.
- Palette: deep ink (#17262A), engraving teal (#0D5E57), aged gold (#8E6A22), paper (#EDF1EE).
- Type: high-contrast serif for headlines (Bodoni-style) + clean sans for body; Farsi: Vazirmatn; Turkish uses the Latin faces (check ğ, ş, ı, İ).
- Format: 4:5 carousels, 9:16 reels, 1:1 or 4:5 single posts. Leave safe margins for RTL Farsi text.

## For each item, provide

1. **Archival image to find** (preferred for history): what object or document, likely source (British Museum, Met, Wikimedia Commons, Smithsonian), and the licence to confirm. Credit line text.
2. **Or AI illustration prompt** when no licensed image exists: describe scene, period-accurate details, engraving or etching style, palette above, composition with text space. Label as "illustration" on the slide if it could be mistaken for a real historical image.
3. **Data visual** where a number is the story: simple bar, line or a "stack" comparison; give the numbers and source.
4. **Per-slide table** for carousels: slide # | visual | on-slide text placement | notes for RTL.

## Licence rule

Prefer collections that allow commercial use: The Met Open Access (CC0), Smithsonian Open Access (CC0), Wikimedia Commons public-domain files, Library of Congress public-domain items. British Museum images are CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial): do not use them on brand channels without permission. Record source, licence and credit line on the card's `visual` field. Never AI-generate sacred text (e.g. the shahada) or real historical scripts; show a real licensed object or leave the text out of the illustration.

Never reproduce real company logos, current banknotes in a way that could be counterfeit-like (show them small, angled, or partial, and never at actual size), or images of identifiable private people.
