# Capacity and scaling

The desk is built for more than today's pace. Scale by changing how many desks are dispatched and how many cards are kept, never by skipping verification.

## Pace levels

| Level | Editions | Desks dispatched per edition | Cards kept in archive per edition | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Base (default)** | 2 / week | 3 desks, rotating | all Verified (typically 8–15) | — |
| Growth | 3–5 / week | 4 desks | all Verified | Weekly "myth-buster" slot from Oddities desk |
| Daily | 7 / week | 3 desks + Wire every day | all Verified | Wire desk runs as a scheduled morning task |
| Full newsroom | 7+ / week + 1 feature / month | all 8 desks weekly | all Verified | Monthly long-form thread / LinkedIn article |

At Base pace the archive grows by roughly 20–30 verified facts a week, so slower weeks can be filled from stock without new research.

## Desk rotation at Base pace

Edition A (early week): Wire OR the desk tied to an anniversary + one history desk (Antiquity / Empires & Crashes) + Oddities & Myths.
Edition B (late week): Cons & Heists OR Crypto + Money Lock + Our Region.

Keep the mix over any 6 editions to at least: 2 eras before 1900, 1 crypto/fintech, 1 con/fraud, 1 regional (Cyprus/Türkiye/Iran/Middle East), 1 myth or oddity.

## Stock mode

When the user says "use the archive" or time is short, skip step 3: pick the best unused Verified card from the archive, re-verify any card older than 6 months (or any Wire card older than 2 weeks), then write.
