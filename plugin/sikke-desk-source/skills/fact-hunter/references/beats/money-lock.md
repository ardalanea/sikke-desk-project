# Beat: Money Lock (when money gets trapped)

**ID prefix:** LCK
**Covers:** bank runs, bank holidays, bail-ins (Cyprus 2013), capital controls (Iceland, Greece 2015, Argentina's corralito 2001, Lebanon from 2019), gold confiscation (US 1933), currency demonetisation (India 2016), sanctions and frozen reserves, dormant accounts and unclaimed fortunes, lost crypto keys, Swiss bank secrecy history.
**Best sources:** central banks, IMF papers, official decrees, established press, academic studies.
**Golden angles:** what it felt like for ordinary depositors, the rules nobody expected, how long it lasted, what changed afterwards.
**Watch out for:** this beat touches politics and real losses. Stay factual and neutral, no blame language, no speculation about future freezes, nothing that could cause panic about any current bank.
