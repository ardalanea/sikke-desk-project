# Beat: Cons & Heists (any era)

**ID prefix:** CON
**Covers:** Ponzi and pyramid schemes, affinity fraud, forgers and counterfeiters (incl. state counterfeiting like Operation Bernhard), famous con artists, bank robberies and heists, insider trading, accounting fraud (Enron, Wirecard), money laundering, rogue traders, how each was caught.
**Best sources:** SEC and DOJ releases, court judgments, FCA/SFO, Ponzitracker, ICIJ, OCCRP, established press.
**Golden angles:** the trick explained simply, the tiny mistake that exposed it, the size in relatable terms, the lesson readers can use to protect themselves.
**Watch out for:** legal status (charged ≠ convicted), living private individuals (escalate), ongoing cases, sensational numbers from press releases vs. court-found amounts. Never write a how-to of a scam; explain the mechanism at the level a victim needs to recognise it.
