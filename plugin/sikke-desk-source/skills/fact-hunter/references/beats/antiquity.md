# Beat: Antiquity (before 1500)

**ID prefix:** ANT
**Covers:** barter and commodity money (grain, cattle, salt, shells, cacao), Mesopotamian temple loans and interest, the Code of Hammurabi on debt, first coins (Lydia), Achaemenid daric, Greek and Roman money and debasement, Sasanian silver, Islamic dinar and dirham, hawala and the Silk Road, Song/Yuan paper money, Knights Templar, Venetian and Florentine banking, the Medici, double-entry bookkeeping.
**Best sources:** British Museum, ANS, Encyclopaedia Iranica, university pages, Federal Reserve History (origins), peer-reviewed numismatics.
**Golden angles:** "first ever" stories, rules that sound modern, money objects that look absurd today, ancient scams and debasements, Iranian and Anatolian firsts (audience pride).
**Watch out for:** precise dates (use "c." and ranges), romantic myths (e.g. "salary comes from salt" is disputed — label it), confusion between legend and evidence.
